Zero Compromise Safety

Our 6-Pillar Driver Vetting Standard

Less than 7% of driver applicants successfully pass our stringent verification and background scrutiny.

1. Criminal & Police Clearance

Exhaustive federal, state, and county criminal background checks performed in partnership with certified national vetting agencies.

2. Driving Record (MVR) History

Complete review of DMV license history spanning the last 7 years. Absolute zero tolerance for DUIs, reckless driving, or license suspensions.

3. 5-Panel Drug & Health Screening

Rigorous medical fitness verification including 20/20 corrected vision test, cardiovascular health checks, and mandatory 5-panel substance screening.

4. Defensive Road Driving Test

In-car practical road evaluations assessing smoothness of acceleration, braking distances, blind-spot checks, and courteous lane discipline.

5. Residential Address Verification

Physical on-site verification of permanent and current residential addresses, accompanied by verified family references and guarantor endorsements.

6. Chauffeur Etiquette Training

Classroom sessions on passenger confidentiality, luggage protocol, smartphone navigation efficiency, and respectful client communication.
